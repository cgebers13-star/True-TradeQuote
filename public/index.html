Good to know

Is this a real quote?

No — it's a rough, non-binding estimate based on typical pricing for similar jobs. Your final price depends on an in-person look from the pro you choose.

Do you book the job for me?

Not in this version. We show you the top-rated pros near you and you reach out directly — no middleman, no pressure.

What do you do with my info?

Nothing is stored or shared with contractors in this version — it's show-only. We don't collect payment info at any point.
